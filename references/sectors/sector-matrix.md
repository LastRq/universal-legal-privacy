# Sector-sensitive audit matrix (not an exhaustive legal catalogue)

| Sector | Confirm at minimum | High-risk review |
|---|---|---|
| Health, dental, clinical | clinical data, patient/guardian relationship, access, retention, confidentiality, hosting, professional obligations | medical/health data, regulated services, professional review |
| Finance, insurance | scope of regulated activities vs software tooling, payment provider, identity verification, fraud, financial data | licensing and regulated-service representations |
| Real estate | lead data, identity documents, property details, buyer/seller/agent roles, marketing consent | intermediary roles, discrimination, contracts |
| E-commerce/subscriptions | merchant vs platform, prices, taxes, payments, recurring billing, refunds, notices | consumer rights, cross-border sales |
| Education and minors | student/parent/teacher roles, age ranges, tracking, consent basis, school contracts | children, profiling |
| Marketplaces/multi-tenant | platform vs seller/controller, recipient data, tenant segregation, support, complaints | multi-entity legal responsibility |
| AI and automation | provider, input/output content, logging, training settings, retention, human oversight | consequential automated decisions, high-risk AI |
| Biometrics, surveillance, location | data necessity, device permissions, inference, recipients, retention | biometric identifiers, continuous tracking |
| Local-first/offline software | local encryption claims verified, backup/sync path, logs, cloud toggles, telemetry | key custody, exports, sync transfers |

For every sector, determine which country-specific regulation applies from verified official sources. Do not treat this table as a list of legal requirements.
