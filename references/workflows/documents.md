# Legal drafting protocol

## Gate

Before drafting, confirm verified legal entity, service description, markets, relevant processing/contract flows, contacts, retention and recipients where relevant. Record real regulatory sources. A missing fact is a question, not a placeholder converted into a declaration. Any template text is illustrative, not a legal conclusion.

## Document selection

- Privacy notice: describe actual data categories, sources, purposes, legal bases where required, recipients, storage and retention, cross-border transfers, rights and verified contact channels.
- Terms of service: service boundaries, acceptable use, purchases, renewal, termination, liability, dispute resolution only when supported by the actual business model and governing law.
- Cookie/storage notice: observed cookie/SDK/storage inventory with categories, durations, vendors, control and withdrawal paths. Do not generate an unnecessary banner solely because the site exists.
- Sectoral notices: health confidentiality, children's information, financial services, education, real-estate brokerage, AI decision-making, biometrics etc. only if relevant.
- Refund, cancellation, subscription, warranty, billing and invoice disclosures: only where activities and rights justify them.
- Processing agreements and vendor terms are separate documents when controller/processor relationships and contract needs are established. Do not invent contractual acceptance by third parties.

## Multi-jurisdiction strategy

Research which specific obligations apply to the actual operator, data subjects, targeting and establishments. Prefer a clear common notice plus jurisdiction-specific sections when compatible; otherwise separate notices and distinguish operating entities. Do not presume translations are adequate for every territory; have required texts reviewed.

## Traceability and versioning

Track document kind, title, draft date, locale, jurisdictions, evidence references, source links and consulted dates. A draft must remain marked "not for publication" until approved. Never auto-generate legal citations from model memory alone. The included local CLI is intentionally limited to evidence-backed illustrative Spanish/English drafts; adapt other languages in the agent only after verified source content and review.

## Existing documents

Compare existing route, footer and text before writing. Preserve human text; propose a diff rather than replace it. Flag any conflict between written terms and actual behavior. The owner approves the final publishing action.
